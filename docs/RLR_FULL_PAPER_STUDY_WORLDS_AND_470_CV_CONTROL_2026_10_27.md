## Publication quality targets and stop rules

- Main **positive-class F1, AUC**, supplementary Macro-F1, BA, logloss, Brier, calibration; RLR50 original vs real kTSP50 is publication's central classification comparison. RLR already has strong original logloss; an improved tree should not just improve logloss on almost perfectly separable inner-V cohorts.
- Selection, all gene-pair feature preparation and active relations in each model must remain train-only. Use original RLR K50 d2 m10 alpha=.5 lambda.min; CFO local fit alpha=.5 lambda=.10 with D-only relational anchor root, D-only rank-pair IDs, OOF logistic offsets fitted only on T and no E-driven hyperparameter search.
- Inner V acceptance: LL gain ≥ .005 vs RLR50 and ≥ .002 vs same nominated-pairs global, BA loss ≤ .02 and Macro-F1 loss ≤ .01; record **number/fraction of genuinely accepted trees** on each dataset and actual active model sizes.
- Every fold uses **identical heldout patient IDs** across real kTSP, TSP, RLR, LR and CFO; preserve native kTSP class labels. Independently verify patient IDs, labels, probabilities, AUC/F1/Brier/logloss and repetition-level E test coverage. Dataset-level aggregation and paired confidence across 11 independent cohorts, not pseudo-independent fold-level p-values.
- If the new tree fails to outperform kTSP or identical-pairs non-tree controls on the frozen primary protocol, original unpublished RLR article remains complete and should not be blocked for another unproductive tree variant.
